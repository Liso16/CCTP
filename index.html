About Autism

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a developmental condition that affects communication, behavior, and social interaction.

  • Impacts social communication
  • Includes repetitive behaviors
  • Appears in early childhood

Screening

Screening questions

Please answer the following:

  1. Does the child Avoid eye contact ?

    2. -Does the child show a tendency in avoiding to make eye contact when being interacted with such as when being spoken to or during communication

  2. Are there repetitive movements ?

    3. -Does the child usually show repetitive movements such as repeating the same actions or Hand-flapping

  3. Are there delayed speech patterns ?

    4. -Whether the child display delayed speech development in comparison to others their age

  4. Does the child look at you when you call their name ?

    5. -Whether the child responds by looking at you when their name is called

  5. Does the child use simple gestures ?

    6. -(e.g. wave goodbye)

  6. Does the child stare at nothing with no apparent purpose ?

    7. -Whether there are times when the child appears to stare blankly and does not interact to what is happening around them

  7. Does the child follow where you're looking ?

    8. -Whether When you look at something, does the child follow the same path and look at the same thing

  8. Does the child make involuntary face or body movemnts ?

    9. -Whether the child makes unintneded body or facial expressions

  9. Does the child show interest in children at play ?

    10. -Whether the child displays signs of interest towards playing with other children

  10. Does the child prefer to do things the same way over and over again ?

    11. -Whether the child shows an interest in doing things repetitively
